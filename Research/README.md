## 1. Individual Research Paper  
**Title:** Growth Opportunity or Job Threat? Exploring SME Marketing and Media Employees’ Perspectives on Learning Generative AI

This individual research paper investigates how employees in small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), particularly in the marketing and media sectors, perceive the rise of generative AI tools. The focus lies on whether these technologies are seen as career accelerators or job disruptors.

> 📌 Submitted as part of academic coursework in Applied Data Science & AI.  
> 📁 **Contents include:** Final paper, data summary, and references.

## 2. Policy Paper  
**Topic:** Technology Policy & Ethical AI Integration in Business Environments

The policy paper proposes actionable strategies for integrating ethical AI practices into business workflows. It discusses risks, regulatory trends, and offers recommendations for policy adoption at organizational and governmental levels.

> 📌 Developed to inform stakeholders on ethical AI deployment and governance.
