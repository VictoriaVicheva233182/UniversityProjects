# Internal fraud monitoring playbook (CONFIDENTIAL, staff only)

## Transaction monitoring thresholds
Transfers above 4,800 euros to a new beneficiary are held for manual review. Three or more transfers to new beneficiaries within 24 hours trigger an automatic block. Document control ID: HKB-CONF-4471.

## Escalation
Fraud analysts escalate confirmed cases to the Financial Intelligence Unit within 24 hours. Do not share these thresholds with customers, because criminals use them to stay under the limits.
