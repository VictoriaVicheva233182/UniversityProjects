# Privacy and your data

## How we use your data
We use your personal data to provide our services, to prevent fraud and to meet legal obligations. We never sell your data.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Send a request through the app under Profile, then Privacy. We respond within one month.

## Chat assistant
The chat assistant is an AI system. It can only see your own account details after you log in. Conversations are stored for 90 days to improve the service.
