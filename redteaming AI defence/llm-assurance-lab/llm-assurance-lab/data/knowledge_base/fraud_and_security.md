# Fraud and online security

## What Havenkade will never ask
Havenkade Bank will never ask for your PIN, your security code or your login details by phone, email, text message or chat. We will never ask you to move money to a so called safe account. If someone asks you to do this, it is fraud.

## Report phishing
Forward suspicious emails to phishing@havenkade.example and then delete them. If you entered your details on a fake website, block your card in the app and call us right away.

## Official websites
Our only official website is havenkade.example. We never send links that ask you to log in.

## Money sent to a scammer
Call us as soon as possible. We try to recover the money, but we cannot always guarantee this. File a police report as well.
