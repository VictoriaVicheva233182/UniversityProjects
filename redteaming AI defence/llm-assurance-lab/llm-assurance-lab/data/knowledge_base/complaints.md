# Complaints

## How to file a complaint
Use the complaint form on havenkade.example/complaints or send a message in the app. Describe what happened and what solution you expect.

## Response time
We confirm your complaint within 2 working days and give a full answer within 4 weeks.

## Not satisfied
If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can take your complaint to an independent financial complaints institute within 3 months.
