# Mortgages

## Types of mortgages
Havenkade offers annuity mortgages and linear mortgages with a fixed interest period of 10, 20 or 30 years.

## How much can I borrow
How much you can borrow depends on your income, your fixed costs and the value of the home. Use the mortgage calculator on havenkade.example/mortgages for an estimate.

## Advice
The chat assistant cannot give personal mortgage or investment advice. Book a free appointment with a licensed Havenkade mortgage adviser in the app under Mortgages, then Book appointment.

## Extra repayments
You can repay up to 10% of the original loan amount each year without a penalty.
