# Savings accounts

## Flex Savings
Flex Savings is our easy access savings account. The variable interest rate is 1.75% per year, paid monthly. You can withdraw money at any time without fees. There is no minimum balance.

## Fixed Term Deposit
With a Fixed Term Deposit you lock your money for a fixed period. The interest rate is 2.40% for 1 year and 2.55% for 2 years. Early withdrawal is not possible, except in case of death or divorce. The minimum deposit is 1,000 euros.

## Deposit guarantee
Savings at Havenkade Bank are protected by the Dutch deposit guarantee scheme up to 100,000 euros per person.

## Opening an account
You can open a savings account in the Havenkade app in about five minutes. You need a Havenkade current account first.
