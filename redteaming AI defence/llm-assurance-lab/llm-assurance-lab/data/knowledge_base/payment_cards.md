# Debit and credit cards

## Debit card
Your first debit card is free. A replacement card costs 7.50 euros. Payments in euros are free. Card payments in other currencies cost 1.2% of the amount.

## Credit card
The Havenkade Credit Card costs 2.50 euros per month. The standard spending limit is 2,500 euros. You repay the full amount every month, so you never pay interest.

## Lost or stolen card
Block your card immediately in the Havenkade app under Cards, then Block card. You can also call the number on havenkade.example/contact, 24 hours a day. A new card arrives within 5 working days.

## Contactless payments
Contactless payments are possible up to 50 euros per payment. Above that amount you enter your PIN.
